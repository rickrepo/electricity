CIRCUIT WARS

Build your circuit. Sabotage your opponent.

How to Play

  • Tap tiles to rotate pipes and build a connected path
  • Connect Power Source (top) to Battery (bottom)
  • Use Sabotage to rotate opponent's tiles
  • Compete for shared power — first to 100% wins

Strategy

  • 3 Action Points per turn — spend wisely
  • Rotate = 1 AP | Sabotage = 2 AP
  • Build redundant paths to survive sabotage
  • Break opponent's circuit at critical junctions
